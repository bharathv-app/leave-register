# Leave Register

A free personal leave register for Tamil Nadu Government servants.

- Earned Leave, Casual Leave, Restricted Holidays and compensatory leave in one place
- Checks every leave against the rules: limits, overlaps, the 10-day rule, EL at credit
- Works offline and installs on Android like an app
- Your data stays on your own phone, and optionally in your own Google Drive. Nothing is stored here.

**Open the app:** https://bharathv-app.github.io/leave-register/
On Android, open it in Chrome, then ⋮ → **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).

Setting up your own copy: see [SETUP.md](SETUP.md).

This is not an official Government tool. Rules change by Government Order; your service register
is the final word.
